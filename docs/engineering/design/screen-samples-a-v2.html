Access and readability

✓Text and controls retain hierarchy at increased text scale without hiding primary actions.

States and recovery

✓Loading, empty, success, error, and offline states preserve context and offer one clear next action.
✓Selection uses gold plus shape and weight; meaning never depends on color alone.
